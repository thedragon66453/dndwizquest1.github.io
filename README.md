this is a level 1 wizard generator. but first:

level = character level.
lvl = spell level.
prepared spells = spells in your list.

**spellcasting:**
*spell slots* - 2 lvl 1 spell slots per day. cast a lvl 1 spell - lose a lvl 1 slot. 0 slots - unable to cast. (per day - regenerates after a long rest)
in future levels, such as level 3, you will have 4 lvl 1 and 2 lvl 2,
and there are some spells that you can cast, lose a lvl 2 spell slot instead of 1, but make the spell stronger!
*cantrips(לחשונים)* - lvl 0 spells. cast them freely and infinitely!
*changing your prepared spells* - whenever you finish a long rest, you can change your list of prepared spells, replacing any of the spells there with another one.
*spellcasting ability* - intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your wizard spell (used to calculate DC and spell attack modifiers).
